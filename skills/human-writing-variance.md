# Human Writing Variance

## Goal
Produce prose that is natural, varied, context-sensitive, and less mechanically uniform while preserving accuracy and clarity.

This skill is **not** for evading AI detectors. It is for better writing.

## Evidence base (2024–2026)
- Zanotto & Aroyehun, **EMNLP 2025**, *Linguistic and Embedding-Based Profiling of Texts Generated by Humans and Large Language Models*: human writing showed greater variation and more diverse semantic content; newer model outputs remained comparatively homogenized. https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1163/
- Bhatt, August & Antoniak, **ACL 2025**, *Research Borderlands*: LLMs tend to homogenize structural, stylistic, rhetorical, and citational norms across research cultures. https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1272/
- Cao et al., **Findings of ACL 2026**, *Temporal Flattening in LLM-Generated Text*: LLM writing trajectories exhibit reduced longitudinal variation compared with human authors. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.682/
- Russell et al., **2026**, *StoryScope: Investigating idiosyncrasies in AI fiction*: narrative structure itself contains strong human-vs-LLM signals, including differences in explicitness, temporal structure, subplot use, and resolution patterns. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03136

## Procedure
1. Identify the 1–3 ideas that deserve most of the space. Do not distribute attention evenly by default.
2. Avoid forcing every answer into the same definition → causes → examples → conclusion template.
3. Vary paragraph and sentence length naturally. Do not create artificial randomness.
4. Prefer concrete mechanisms, examples, quantities, and observations over generic decorative prose.
5. Do not restate the thesis after every section. Let evidence carry part of the meaning.
6. Use transitions only when they improve comprehension; avoid repetitive signposting.
7. Preserve legitimate ambiguity, competing explanations, and unresolved trade-offs when the evidence is mixed.
8. Match the rhetorical culture of the task: scientific prose, business analysis, narrative, email, explanation, etc. should not all sound alike.
9. Avoid automatic moral lessons or perfectly closed endings unless the content warrants them.
10. Before finalizing, ask: **Would a careful human writer really organize this topic this evenly?** If not, redistribute emphasis.

## Creative / narrative mode
- Vary scene duration and information density.
- Permit delayed information, recontextualization, and side threads when useful.
- Avoid resolving every loose end.
- Let characters and actions imply meaning instead of explaining every theme.

## Scientific / technical override
Naturalness never overrides precision, source fidelity, reproducibility, safety, or the user’s requested structure.

# Cognivera — Brand Guide

## Brand essence

**Cognivera** is an AI quality and reasoning layer built from research-backed skills.

It sits between a user's request and the final answer, helping an AI decide when to research, verify, question, revise, qualify uncertainty, and improve the final writing.

## Tagline

**Reason better. Verify first. Write for humans.**

Short alternative:

**Evidence into intelligence.**

## One-sentence pitch

Cognivera turns recent AI research into modular skills that make assistants more evidence-aware, reliable, context-sensitive, and natural to read.

## Elevator pitch

Language models are often judged by the quality of their final answer, but many failures happen earlier: the wrong information was selected, an unsupported assumption survived, an error was not localized, or confidence exceeded the evidence. Cognivera treats answer quality as a pipeline. It supplies reusable skills for verification, factual auditing, uncertainty, long-context reading, clarification, orchestration, and human-oriented writing.

## Personality

The brand should feel:

- rigorous, not academic for its own sake;
- intelligent, not mystical;
- calm, not hype-driven;
- transparent about uncertainty;
- technical enough for builders, readable enough for non-specialists.

Avoid language such as “perfect AI”, “zero hallucinations”, “human-level truth engine”, or other claims the research cannot support.

## Visual direction

### Concept
A layered prism or interlocking lens system: raw model output enters one side; evidence, verification, and context refine it; a clearer answer exits the other side.

### Suggested palette
- Deep graphite — foundation / technical rigor
- Off-white — clarity
- Teal or cyan accent — verification / signal
- Warm amber accent — uncertainty / caution

These are conceptual directions, not fixed hex values.

### Typography direction
Use a clean modern sans-serif for the product name and documentation. Technical diagrams should remain minimal and high-contrast.

## Logo idea

A simple mark made from **three nested C-shaped layers** or a stylized lens:

1. outer layer = context;
2. middle layer = verification;
3. inner point = evidence-backed answer.

The mark should still work as a monochrome GitHub avatar.

## Naming system

Use lowercase kebab-case for skill filenames:

`evidence-first-verification.md`

Use title case for displayed names:

**Evidence-First Verification**

Future grouped modules can use:

- Cognivera Verify
- Cognivera Research
- Cognivera Context
- Cognivera Write
- Cognivera Orchestrate

## Messaging

### Primary message
AI quality improves when the process improves.

### Supporting messages
- Verification is different from self-confidence.
- Long answers need claim-level discipline.
- Uncertainty should be communicated, not hidden.
- Context must be reconstructed before it can be reasoned over.
- Natural writing is a final quality layer, not a substitute for correctness.

## Audience

Primary:
- AI power users;
- agent builders;
- researchers;
- developers using Claude Code, Codex-style tools, or local LLMs.

Secondary:
- analysts;
- scientific users;
- content and research teams using AI for long-form work.
